# LinkedIn profile optimisation for Muhammad Karim

Consistency first. The LinkedIn profile, the Master CV (Google Doc id 1RUyNxAt_kLziKvBjeu1NjWZ72dpMOFZuKKflcA1bscI), the tailored CVs and muhammadkarim.co.uk must say the same things: titles, dates, numbers and the descriptor for TRT World ("Head of Audience Growth (led TRT World's digital marketing function)").

Precedence: the career coach's rules and the Job Brief drafting rules come first. Wording follows his voice guide (/topics/writing-voice.md): plain, British, no em-dashes, no exclamation marks.

## 1. Headline

- Maximum 8 words of core role or target skill area. Clear and specific, no keyword dump.
- Use the true title or a truthful descriptor. Do not claim a title he has not held.

## 2. Photo and banner

- Face fills about 30% to 60% of the frame, soft natural front light, clutter-free background, attire suited to the target sector.
- Banner reinforces credibility: project work, a presentation or a relevant professional setting.

## 3. About section

- Use the profile statement structure: who I am, what I do, what I'm looking for.
- Include up to two numbers from the Master CV.
- Use the target skills naturally in sentences, only where they are true.
- Keep it readable: short paragraphs, no keyword lists.

## 4. Skills

- Fill the skills section with real, complementary skills he can defend. Do not list skills he cannot evidence.
- Embed the same skills in the About text and in the relevant experience descriptions, so the skills are backed by context.

## 5. Experience

- Mirror the Master CV's role entries and bullets, condensed for LinkedIn. Titles, employers, dates and numbers exactly as in the Master CV.
- Lead each role with its headline result or scope.
- Add a short line on scope: budget, team size, markets, senior stakeholders.
- Use "led", "contributed to" and "part of a team" accurately.

## 6. Job-search settings

- Open to Work: use the private recruiter-only setting while he is in contract work, so that recruiters see it without a public badge. Revisit once he is free to start.
- Each week, make a small real edit to the profile text, so recruiter searches that favour recent updates see it. Do not make changes just for the sake of it.

## 7. Claims to treat with care

Some LinkedIn statistics in the source guide (search taxonomy size, recruiter-mode multipliers, outreach response rates, a URL parameter trick) are unsourced. Do not build a rule on them until they are verified. Keep practice to what is consistent and honest.

## 8. Checks before it goes live

- Every title, date and number checked against the Master CV.
- Headline 8 words or fewer.
- Portfolio, CV and LinkedIn all agree.
- Spelling, British English, no em-dashes or exclamation marks.
